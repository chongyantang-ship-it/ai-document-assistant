Assessment 3: Real World Applications of Artificial Intelligence

Purpose:
In Assessment 3 students focus on how to apply AI techniques to solve real-world problems, showcasing theoretical knowledge, collaboration, and practical implementation.

Type:
Report and Oral Presentation.

Groupwork:
Group assignment with individually assessed contributions.

Weighting:
40%.

Length:
Up to 3500 words, including references. Use 11-point Times or Arial fonts.

Similarity checks:
Similarity checks on the report will be done by Turnitin. The maximum allowed similarity score is 20%, including quoted material and excluding references.

Submission format:
Report submission: PDF. Only one member from the group needs to submit the report.
The first page must clearly list all group members' names and student IDs.
Please also submit the PowerPoint slide file along with the report submission.

Presentation session:
8 minutes total, including a 7-minute presentation plus 1-minute Q&A.
The PowerPoint file should be submitted before the class presentation session.

Submission Due:
Report due in Week 13, 16 May 2026.
Presentation due in Week 12 for Group A and Week 13 for Group B.

Task Overview:
This is a group assignment. Each group member must clearly document their contributions at the end of the report.
The assessment requires application of AI techniques, critical evaluation, comparative analysis, and research-informed decision-making.

Objective:
Identify a real-world problem that can be effectively addressed using artificial intelligence techniques.
The solution must demonstrate:
- Integration of multiple AI paradigms.
- Strong theoretical justification.
- Rigorous empirical evaluation.
- Critical reflection on limitations, trade-offs, and scalability.

Example domains:
- Intelligent document processing.
- Decision support systems.
- Recommendation systems.
- Autonomous agents or optimisation problems.

Report Sections:
The report should include Introduction, Theoretical Justification, Workflow and Empirical Analysis, Critical Reflection, GitHub Repository, Conclusion, Individual Contributions, and References.
