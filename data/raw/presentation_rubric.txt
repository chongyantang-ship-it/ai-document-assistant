Presentation Rubric: HD Criteria

Content and Relevance:
HD requires a highly relevant real-world problem and AI solution with strong alignment between the problem and chosen methods. The presentation should clearly explain significance, impact and ethical considerations while staying within the time limit.

AI Method Explanation and Justification:
HD requires a clear and concise explanation of the AI-based method with deep understanding. The presentation should justify the selected methods, compare them with alternatives where useful, and explain trade-offs convincingly.

Findings, Contributions and Limitations:
HD requires insightful findings and contributions with clear empirical results that show effectiveness and innovation. Limitations should be critically analysed, including ethical implications.

Presentation Delivery and Interaction:
HD requires engaging, confident and well-paced delivery with effective visuals and clear articulation. Responses to questions should be accurate, insightful and demonstrate deep understanding of the project and its ethical implications.
