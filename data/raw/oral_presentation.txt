Assessment 3: Real World Applications of Artificial Intelligence - Oral Presentation

In-class assessment. Oral presentation of Assignment 3 group project.

Submission:
Please submit the group's PowerPoint presentation before the in-class presentation.
Only one member from the group needs to submit the presentation file.

PowerPoint Presentation Filename:
AT3_Slide_Group_xxxx.pptx, where xxxx is the Group Number.

First slide requirements:
The first slide must clearly list:
1. Group Number
2. All group members' names
3. Student IDs

Presentation:
Each group will deliver a presentation to demonstrate their proposed solution and project outcomes.
All members of the group are required to be present for the group presentation.

The presentation should:
- Highlight the real-world problem.
- Explain the AI-based approach.
- Present findings.
- Explain individual and group contributions.
- Be prepared to answer questions from the class about the problem, solution, and project insights.
